# GUMFALL Game Design Bible

## Vision

GUMFALL is a physical action RPG in which combatants are living gummy creatures.
Characters stretch, deform, crystallize, melt, become pinned, lose limbs, and
continue fighting through class-specific fallback systems.

The combat result is determined by authoritative gameplay simulation. Visual
deformation and procedural presentation represent that result but do not decide it.

## Design Pillars

1. Immediate and responsive controls
2. Directional, physically readable combat
3. Localized body and armor damage
4. Anatomy-driven monster encounters
5. Source-grounded equipment and loot
6. Strong class and lineage identities
7. Discoverable secrets without arbitrary guessing
8. Competitive progression with bounded combat power
9. Fair structured PvP and dangerous opt-in open PvP
10. Long-term expansion through hidden world systems

## Major Systems

- GelFlow combat
- Gummy body integrity
- Armor coverage and weapon materials
- Natural enchantments and defensive Wards
- Character classes and specialties
- Gummy lineages, compositions, and flavor affinities
- Monster anatomy, ecology, roles, and ranks
- Competitive Level 1–100 progression
- Legacy Reroll progression
- Secret post-Level-100 progression
- Secret mazes and monster royalty
- Structured arenas and tournament events
- Shattercrown open-PvP city
- Monster Ascension tournaments

## Documentation Rule

Public documentation may explain that secret systems exist, but it must not expose
their complete solutions. Exact requirements and unreleased expansion data belong
only in protected private design storage.
