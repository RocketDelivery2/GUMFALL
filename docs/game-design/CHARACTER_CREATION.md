## 1. Creation Flow Overview

Character creation in GUMFALL is a sequential selection process that establishes the player character's physical nature, cultural origin, and starting capabilities. No selection is arbitrary — every choice has mechanical consequences that persist through the full game.

**Creation Order:**
1. Lineage (who you are physically — anatomy, movement, limb layout)
2. Composition (how your gel is structured — material properties)
3. Flavor Affinity (your elemental/energetic resonance)
4. Background (where you came from — starting skills and story hooks)
5. Class (what you do — see [Classes and Specialties](CLASSES_AND_SPECIALTIES.md))
6. Attribute Allocation (fine-tuning your starting stats)
7. Appearance Customization (within lineage parameters)
8. Name

**Design Philosophy:** The player makes meaningful choices before touching a class. The physical body comes first because in GUMFALL, the body is the game's core system. A Bearkin who is Firm-Set with Cherry Ember affinity plays differently from a Bearkin who is Aerated with Blue Frost affinity — not dramatically, but perceptibly.

## 2. Lineage Selection

All 12 lineages are available at creation. Lineage determines:
- Physical anatomy (body region layout)
- Movement type (ground, climb, burrow, etc.)
- Limb layout (how many, which are reattachable)
- Reattachment rules (time, method, range)
- Primary attribute tendency (soft bonus, not a cap)
- Composition preference (some compositions are uncommon for some lineages)
- Equipment accommodation (some weapons/armor require modification)
- Starting benefits and tradeoffs

Full detail in [Lineages, Compositions, Flavors](LINEAGES_COMPOSITIONS_FLAVORS.md).

### 2.1 Bearkin

**Physical Description:** Large, bear-shaped gummy creatures. Broad shoulders, substantial limb mass. Immediately recognizable silhouette. Range from cub-size to very large depending on composition.

**Anatomy:** Standard bipedal layout — head, torso, two arms, two legs. Tail optional (cosmetic).

**Movement Type:** Ground movement primary. Moderate climb. No swim or burrow without equipment.

**Limb Layout:** 4 limbs (2 arms, 2 legs) + head. All reattachable. Tail (if cosmetic) is also reattachable but provides no mechanical benefit.

**Reattachment Rules:** Within standard range (arm's length + 1 body length). Standard reattachment time. Bearkin composition tends toward Firm-Set or Soft-Set, making reattachment slightly easier than Crystal-Set lineages.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Might, Fortitude

**Composition Preference:** Firm-Set (most common), Soft-Set (common), Aerated (uncommon)

**Cultural Identity:** Bearkin cultures span the full political spectrum of the Sweetlands. No single Bearkin cultural identity — they are the most distributed lineage.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Standard equipment fits Bearkin without modification. Oversized variants exist for the largest Bearkin.

**Starting Benefit:** +1 effective Might at character creation (non-permanent — reflects starting physical condition).

**Tradeoffs:** Larger target profile means enemies more easily hit them. Not the fastest lineage.

### 2.2 Wormkin

**Physical Description:** Long, segmented gummy creatures. No fixed arm/leg distinction — segments can develop limb-equivalents based on composition and development. Highly flexible.

**Anatomy:** Segmented — head segment + N body segments (PROPOSED: 6–12 depending on individual). Limbs develop from segments.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement (undulation), excellent in confined spaces. Burrow available with appropriate equipment.

**Limb Layout:** Limbs emerge from lateral segments. Can have 0–4 developed limbs depending on individual (cosmetic choice at creation). All developed limbs are reattachable. Tail segment always reattachable.

**Reattachment Rules:** Wormkin can reattach at longer range due to their flexible body structure. Segments can also partially reattach — a lost mid-section can be recovered and reattached in pieces.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Technique

**Composition Preference:** Soft-Set (most common), Syrup-Core (uncommon)

**Cultural Identity:** Wormkin culture emphasizes patience, underground networks, and long memory. The Licoricefolk underground networks are largely maintained by Wormkin labor and expertise.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Most standard equipment requires modification for Wormkin due to non-standard body shape. Dedicated Wormkin equipment lines exist in most major markets.

**Starting Benefit:** +1 effective Agility at character creation. Reattachment range extended.

**Tradeoffs:** Specialized equipment (more expensive, less available in remote areas). Upright stance not natural — some elevation advantages unavailable.

### 2.3 Ringborn

**Physical Description:** Torus-shaped gummy creatures — circular or oval ring cross-sections. Can see in multiple directions. Distinctive silhouette.

**Anatomy:** Ring body — no distinct head/torso separation in the traditional sense. Eyes and sensory organs distributed around the ring. Limbs extend from the ring.

**Movement Type:** Rolling movement primary (very fast on flat terrain), upright walking secondary. Can rotate ring to present different aspects.

**Limb Layout:** 2–4 limbs extending from ring body. All reattachable. The ring itself is a single body region — damage to it cannot be reattached piecemeal.

**Reattachment Rules:** Limbs reattach normally. Ring damage heals at Sugar Wells only (cannot reattach a broken ring section in the field).

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Spirit

**Composition Preference:** Crystal-Set (common), Firm-Set (common)

**Cultural Identity:** Ringborn are often associated with formal structures and hierarchy — their anatomy suggests completeness and cycles. Many Ringborn nobility exist within the Crown Accord.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Armor must be custom-fitted for ring anatomy. Weapon use is standard (limbs hold weapons normally).

**Starting Benefit:** Rolling movement available in combat — can execute a roll-dodge that covers more distance than standard dodge.

**Tradeoffs:** Ring anatomy means torso damage is severe (no torso/limb distinction to absorb separately). Custom armor expensive.

### 2.4 Gumdrop Folk

**Physical Description:** Classic teardrop-shaped gummy creatures. The most traditional "gummy" silhouette — rounded base, pointed top. Short, compact, deceptively sturdy.

**Anatomy:** Single-body teardrop — head is the pointed top, torso is the full rounded body. Limbs extend from the lower sides.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement. Short legs mean lower top speed but exceptional stability (low center of gravity).

**Limb Layout:** 2 arms, 2 legs (short). All reattachable. Head (pointed top) is a distinct region — reattachable.

**Reattachment Rules:** Standard reattachment. Aerated composition common among Gumdrop Folk means they bounce back from impacts — limb separation happens more easily but reattachment is also faster.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Fortitude, Spirit

**Composition Preference:** Aerated (most common), Soft-Set (common)

**Cultural Identity:** Gumdrop Folk communities are warm, communal, and storytelling-oriented. Strong oral tradition. Widespread across the Sweetlands — found in every region.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Short stature means some standard weapon sizes require modification. Abundant off-the-shelf Gumdrop Folk equipment.

**Starting Benefit:** Enhanced stability — harder to knock down or stagger. Aerated composition resonance means bouncing from falls takes less body integrity damage.

**Tradeoffs:** Shorter reach in melee. Lower top running speed.

### 2.5 Jellybean Folk

**Physical Description:** Oval, smooth-surfaced gummy creatures. Slightly flattened cross-section. Come in a wide range of colors associated with their flavor affinity.

**Anatomy:** Smooth ovoid body — head and torso continuous, with a subtle distinction. Limbs are thinner and more fluid-looking than other lineages.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement with exceptional lateral agility. Quick direction changes.

**Limb Layout:** 2 arms, 2 legs. All reattachable. Head region is semi-distinct (partially integrated with torso, meaning head loss is less catastrophically destabilizing but also harder to fully reattach).

**Reattachment Rules:** Fast standard reattachment. Crystal-Set Jellybean Folk have slightly slower reattachment (more brittle separation).

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Intellect

**Composition Preference:** Crystal-Set (common), Layered (uncommon)

**Cultural Identity:** Jellybean Folk are associated with mercurial energy and emotional transparency. Also associated with the academic world — many Flavorweavers are Jellybean Folk.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Standard equipment fits well. Jellybean Folk are close enough to "standard" proportions to use most equipment without modification.

**Starting Benefit:** Lateral agility bonus — dodge to the side covers more distance, costs less Stamina.

**Tradeoffs:** Lighter than average — more susceptible to Crush damage and knockback.

---

### 2.6 Bottleborn

**Physical Description:** Bottle-shaped gummy creatures — narrow neck, wider body below. Often partially transparent (the bottle material quality varies). Some Bottleborn have visible swirling gel interiors.

**Anatomy:** The neck is structurally fragile but flexible. The body below is the main torso. Limbs extend from the shoulder area below the neck.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement. The neck structure allows unusual head mobility (270° rotation possible).

**Limb Layout:** 2 arms, 2 legs. All reattachable. The neck/head connection is the most vulnerable structural point — designed around rather than ignored.

**Reattachment Rules:** Neck reattachment requires special technique (Gelomancer or Sugar Well for full restoration). Limb reattachment standard.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Intellect, Technique

**Composition Preference:** Syrup-Core (common), Layered (common)

**Cultural Identity:** Bottleborn culture is strongly associated with trade, accumulation, and mercantile systems. The major trade networks of the Sweetlands pre-Shattering were largely organized by Bottleborn houses.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Neck armor is specialized. Otherwise standard.

**Starting Benefit:** Intellect resonance — enhanced focus generation. Neck mobility gives awareness bonus (slightly wider detection arc).

**Tradeoffs:** Neck region is structurally vulnerable. Neck armor is expensive and rare.

---

### 2.7 Sourlings

**Physical Description:** Visibly sour-surface gummy creatures — slightly crystalline outer layer from sour coating, rougher texture than smooth-gel lineages. Tart visual quality.

**Anatomy:** Bipedal, humanoid proportions. The sour coating is a real structural layer over the gel interior.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement. No special mobility.

**Limb Layout:** Standard 2 arms, 2 legs. All reattachable. The sour coating on separated limbs makes them slightly harder to accidentally stick to the wrong surface during reattachment.

**Reattachment Rules:** Slightly extended reattachment window due to the sour-coating layer acting as mild structural stabilizer. Reattachment in Sour-type environments is faster.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Fortitude, Technique

**Composition Preference:** Firm-Set (common), Crystal-Set (uncommon)

**Cultural Identity:** Sourling culture has a reputation for endurance, dark humor, and comfort with difficulty. Communities in the Sourswamp Delta have survived conditions that would destroy most lineages.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Standard equipment fits. Sour-resistant material preferences exist.

**Starting Benefit:** Sour damage resistance. Sour status effects accumulate more slowly. Stamina recovery slightly faster under physical stress.

**Tradeoffs:** Sour coating means some adhesion benefits are reduced (hard to stick to surfaces intentionally). Sourlings are not loved by all cultures.

---

### 2.8 Licoricefolk

**Physical Description:** Long, dark, flexible gummy creatures with a distinctly different texture from other lineages — licorice rather than standard fruit-gel. Range from thin and ropy to thick and twisted.

**Anatomy:** Highly variable — Licoricefolk body plans span from near-bipedal to multi-limbed to sinuous near-limbless forms. The standard template is bipedal-adjacent.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement with exceptional constriction ability. Can occupy smaller spaces than size suggests (licorice compresses differently than standard gel).

**Limb Layout:** 2 standard arms, 2 standard legs. Additional partial limbs (prehensile sections) possible (cosmetic choice). Standard limbs reattachable. Prehensile sections regenerate.

**Reattachment Rules:** Limbs reattach at standard rate, but reattachment in dark conditions is slightly better (Licoricefolk sense their own body parts by resonance).

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Intellect

**Composition Preference:** Soft-Set (most common), Layered (uncommon)

**Cultural Identity:** Licoricefolk culture values privacy, layered trust, and complex social structures. The underground networks are associated with Licoricefolk maintenance — not because they're secretive (though they often are) but because they have the physical and organizational tools for it.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Licorice-specific armor exists and provides unique properties. Most standard armor requires modification.

**Starting Benefit:** Constriction — enhanced grapple/bind ability. Darkness navigation bonus. Compression movement (squeeze through tight spaces).

**Tradeoffs:** Licorice composition means some elemental interactions differ from gel standards. Some Flavor affinities have unusual interactions with licorice substrate.

### 2.9 Marshfolk

**Physical Description:** Large, low-density gummy creatures. Swamp-adapted body plan — wider stance, water-resistant outer layer, adapted for wet and unstable terrain.

**Anatomy:** Broad-based bipedal layout. Wide feet/base for stability in soft terrain. Slightly shorter than average.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement with exceptional performance in wet, soft, or unstable terrain (marshes, syrup bogs, unstable sugar sediment). Swimming in viscous fluids.

**Limb Layout:** 2 arms, 2 legs (wide-stance). All reattachable. Feet/legs separated in bog terrain can be recovered from the surface of the material.

**Reattachment Rules:** Standard reattachment, with bonus recovery speed in wet environments.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Fortitude, Might

**Composition Preference:** Soft-Set (most common), Syrup-Core (uncommon)

**Cultural Identity:** Marshfolk are literal, practically-minded, and deeply community-bound within their own communities. Distrust of abstract systems — preference for observable, physical evidence.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Standard equipment with water-resistant treatment preferred. Bog-specific equipment variants exist.

**Starting Benefit:** Terrain mastery — no movement penalty in wet/bog terrain. Additional Stability in low-ground environments.

**Tradeoffs:** Outside their terrain type, Marshfolk lose their terrain advantage. Social navigation of urban/formal environments is often awkward (cultural rather than mechanical).

### 2.10 Jawbreaker Kin

**Physical Description:** Small, nearly spherical, extremely hard exterior gummy creatures. The outer shell is crystallized to near-stone hardness. Interior is softer.

**Anatomy:** Spherical body — head and torso functionally unified. Limbs extend from the sphere. Small relative to most lineages.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement, rolling (slower than Ringborn but more precise). Excellent at holding position.

**Limb Layout:** 2 arms, 2 legs (short, strong). All reattachable. The hard exterior means limb separation requires more force — but once separated, the limb is harder to damage further.

**Reattachment Rules:** Reattachment requires more sustained contact time due to hard exterior. Softer compositions (rare for Jawbreaker Kin) reattach faster.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Fortitude, Stability (derived)

**Composition Preference:** Crystal-Set (most common), Firm-Set (common)

**Cultural Identity:** Jawbreaker Kin cultures value endurance and terse competence. Speech is efficient. Respect is demonstrated through action rather than words.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Standard equipment often requires small-scale modification. Jawbreaker Kin armor is among the most protective per-weight-class in the Sweetlands.

**Starting Benefit:** Shell Defense — natural armor value from hard exterior. Enhanced resistance to crystallizing status effects.

**Tradeoffs:** Lower natural Agility tendency. Hard exterior can interfere with Adhesion-based mechanics.

### 2.11 Drakegums

**Physical Description:** Large, scale-patterned, draconic gummy creatures. The scale pattern is a physical surface texture — raised crystalline-gel scales over a gel interior. Imposing silhouette.

**Anatomy:** Large bipedal with vestigial or developed wings (cosmetic choice — wings are non-functional at base, flight requires abilities), tail, and scale-plated body.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement primary. Wings (if cosmetic): glide from heights only (not powered flight). Tail provides balance.

**Limb Layout:** 2 arms, 2 legs, tail, optional wings. All standard limbs reattachable. Tail reattachable. Wings do not reattach — they regenerate at Sugar Wells.

**Reattachment Rules:** Scale exterior slightly complicates reattachment (harder to bond surfaces). Standard reattachment time + 15% (PROPOSED value).

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Might, Spirit

**Composition Preference:** Layered (most common), Crystal-Set (common)

**Cultural Identity:** Drakegum cultures have strong noble traditions and territorial pride. The Ashglass Wyrm Throne is a Drakegum political institution.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Scale-compatible armor required. Specialized Drakegum equipment is expensive and prestigious.

**Starting Benefit:** Scale Resistance — natural armor effect against Slice and Pierce damage. Spirit resonance for Flavorweaver-adjacent abilities.

**Tradeoffs:** Largest target profile among standard lineages. Wing cosmetics restrict some armor options. Most expensive default equipment.

### 2.12 Swarmkin

**Physical Description:** A Swarmkin is a colony of smaller gummy entities operating as a single person. The colony can be loose (individual members visible, some independent movement) or tight (compressed into a roughly humanoid shape).

**Anatomy:** Colony anatomy — the concept of "body region" applies to the colony's distributed body. Head is the densest concentration of colony members. Losing large numbers of colony members is the Swarmkin equivalent of limb loss.

**Movement Type:** Ground movement (humanoid shape), swarm dispersal (spread out to cover area or flow through gaps), flight (in dispersed form — limited range and duration). Unique movement type.

**Limb Layout:** Limb equivalents are formed by colony concentration. "Limb loss" is colony-member loss. Swarmkin recover colony members from within range rather than reattaching severed limbs — recovery rules are different. Colony members regenerate at Sugar Wells.

**Reattachment Rules:** Not standard reattachment — colony recovery. Members within range automatically return. Sugar Well restores full colony count.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Spirit

**Composition Preference:** All compositions apply differently — the colony composition is the average of member compositions. Aerated (common), Soft-Set (common).

**Cultural Identity:** Swarmkin culture operates on consensus and collective voice. Individual Swarmkin express individual personality while also being part of a larger whole.

**Equipment Accommodations:** Conventional armor does not fit. Swarmkin-specific equipment exists.

**Starting Benefit:** Colony dispersal — can flow through small gaps, cover area effects, resist single-target damage (it hits one colony member, not the whole). Unique mobility.

**Tradeoffs:** Equipment is specialized and less available. Social systems built for singular individuals can be confusing. Extreme cold or crystallization effects affect the whole colony.

## 3. Composition Selection

Six compositions, selected after lineage. Full detail in [Lineages, Compositions, Flavors](LINEAGES_COMPOSITIONS_FLAVORS.md).

| Composition | Structure | Key Mechanic | Tradeoff |
|-------------|-----------|--------------|----------|
| Soft-Set | Flexible, standard gel | Best deformation recovery, good reattachment | Lower natural structure |
| Firm-Set | Denser, stiffer gel | Better impact resistance, slower deformation | Slower reattachment |
| Crystal-Set | Crystallized outer layer | High structure rating, strong vs. Slice | Brittle; Cold and Crush cause shattering |
| Syrup-Core | Fluid interior | Unique flow mechanics, fast reattachment | Lowest structure, unstable under heavy impact |
| Layered | Distinct material layers | Outer/inner damage tracking, outer layer absorbs | Layer removal changes properties |
| Aerated | Gas-bubble-infused gel | Lighter weight, bounce recovery, Crush resistance | Heat vulnerability; limb separation easier |

## 6. Attribute Allocation Rules

After Background selection, players allocate a starting pool of attribute points (PROPOSED: 6 points at creation).

**Rules:**
- No attribute may start below 1.
- No attribute may start above 4 at character creation.
- Background provides soft bonuses that affect attribute effectiveness but do not count toward the cap.
- Lineage attribute tendencies provide +1 effective to tendency attributes for the first 10 levels (PROPOSED).

**Primary Attributes:**
1. **Might** — Physical force generation, melee damage, carrying capacity
2. **Agility** — Speed, precision, dodge effectiveness, ranged accuracy
3. **Fortitude** — Body integrity maintenance, status resistance, physical endurance
4. **Technique** — Weapon mastery speed, crafting quality, combat precision
5. **Intellect** — Ability learning speed, lore knowledge, crafting diversity
6. **Spirit** — Spiritual/elemental power, Ward strength, Conviction resource

See [Attributes and Stats](ATTRIBUTES_AND_STATS.md) for full detail.

## 7. Starting Equipment Defaults

Starting equipment is determined by Background selection (see Section 5) and modified by class selection.

**Universal starting items:**
- One primary weapon (Background-determined or Class-determined, whichever is more specific)
- One set of starting armor (Background-determined)
- Small currency amount (Background-determined)
- Basic consumables (PROPOSED: 3 Standard Gel-Wrap bandages)

**Class overrides:** Some classes provide their own starting equipment that supersedes or supplements Background equipment.

## 8. Appearance Customization

Within the lineage's physical parameters, players may customize:
- Color (within the lineage's color range — flavor affinity influences available colors)
- Composition visual quality (translucency, surface texture)
- Scar configuration (pre-existing cosmetic damage)
- Limb configuration (where applicable — cosmetic limb count, shape)
- Face design (eyes, expression baseline)
- Size (within lineage range — PROPOSED: ±20% from lineage average)

**Armor appearance:** Starting armor appearance is set by Background. Players may acquire cosmetic armor variants throughout the game.

## 9. Accessibility — Creation UI

The character creation interface should accommodate:

- **Screen reader support:** All selectable options must have descriptive text labels, not just visual icons.
- **Color blindness:** Lineage and flavor color options must have non-color identifiers (name, pattern).
- **One-handed navigation:** All creation screens must be navigable with a single input device.
- **Creation preview:** All choices should show a live preview of the character's appearance.
- **Undo options:** All selections should be reversible until the player confirms creation.
- **Information depth:** A "simplified" view (key information only) and a "detailed" view (all mechanical implications) should be toggleable.

---

## 10. New Player Guidance

The game should guide new players through character creation with the following support:

- **Lineage recommendations:** Brief "if you want to play like X, consider Y" suggestions at lineage selection. Not prescriptive — informative.
- **Composition warnings:** If a player selects a lineage/composition combination with significant tradeoffs, a brief note should appear (not a warning, a note: "Jawbreaker Kin Crystal-Set characters have the highest natural armor but the slowest reattachment").
- **Class preview:** Before confirming class, show the class resource and one example ability.
- **"Try first" option (PROPOSED):** A short demo encounter available from the character creation screen that lets the player test movement and basic combat before committing.
- **No dead ends:** No creation combination should produce an unplayable character. All combinations are viable; some are specialized.
