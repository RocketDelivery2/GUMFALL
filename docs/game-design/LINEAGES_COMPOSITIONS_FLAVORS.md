## 1. Overview

Lineage, Composition, and Flavor Affinity are the three foundational physical descriptors of a GUMFALL character. Together they determine:
- What the body looks like and how it moves
- How the body responds to damage, status effects, and environment
- What elemental resonances the body has
- What equipment accommodations are required
- What cultural and social contexts the character comes from

These three choices interact — a Bearkin (Lineage) who is Crystal-Set (Composition) with Blue Frost affinity (Flavor) is a qualitatively different physical entity from a Bearkin who is Aerated with Cherry Ember.

## 2. Lineages — Full Detail

### 2.1 Bearkin

**Physical Appearance:** Large, round-limbed, bear-shaped gummy creatures. Their surface has a slight translucency typical of standard gel. Colors range widely but Cherry Ember (red/orange/warm) is the most statistically common affinity. Face is expressive, with round ears and wide-set eyes.

**Anatomy:** Standard bipedal. Head, neck, torso, two arms, two legs. The torso is the largest body region. No tail by default (cosmetic option available).

**Movement Type:** Ground bipedal. Moderate speed. Can walk upright or drop to all-fours for short bursts of faster movement (PROPOSED: +20% speed, -dodge option). Moderate climbing ability.

**Limb Layout:**
- Head (1) — reattachable
- Torso (1) — not separable
- Left arm (1) — reattachable
- Right arm (1) — reattachable
- Left leg (1) — reattachable
- Right leg (1) — reattachable

**Reattachment Rules:** Standard range (within 1.5 body-lengths). Standard bond time. Bearkin Adhesion tends toward moderate — enough to reattach reliably, not fast enough to reattach under combat pressure without a moment of stillness.

**Cultural Identity:** Bearkin are distributed across the Sweetlands without a dominant cultural center. They appear in every political faction, every class, every region. The red Bearkin of the Cherry Ember tradition have a slight association with military service (Sugar Guard history), but this is cultural, not biological.

**Home Region:** No single home — widely distributed. Cherry Ember Bearkin culturally cluster in the Fractured Flats (post-Shattering).

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Might (+1 effective first 10 levels), Fortitude (+1 effective first 10 levels)

**Composition Preference:** Firm-Set (most common), Soft-Set (common), Aerated (uncommon), Crystal-Set (rare but notable)

**Equipment Accommodations:** Standard sizing. Oversized variants exist for composition-driven larger Bearkin. No special modifications required.

**Special Rules:** None beyond standard.

**Starting Benefits:** +1 effective Might. Physical stagger duration reduced by 10% (PROPOSED).

**Tradeoffs:** Larger target profile — enemy body-region targeting benefits from the wider surface area. Lower default Agility than smaller lineages.

### 2.2 Wormkin

**Physical Appearance:** Long, segmented gummy creatures. Segments are separated by slightly narrower connective tissue. Color runs the full spectrum — often multicolored (stripes across segments). Limbs emerge from lateral segments as needed.

**Anatomy:** Segmented chain — head segment, variable number of body segments (6–12 typical), tail segment. Body regions:
- Head segment
- Anterior trunk (first 2–3 body segments)
- Mid trunk (middle segments)
- Posterior trunk (last 2–3 segments)
- Tail segment

**Movement Type:** Undulation on ground. Excellent in confined spaces. Burrow with appropriate equipment.

**Limb Layout:** Head (reattachable), developed limbs from lateral segments (0–4, reattachable), tail (reattachable). Individual segments can separate — mid-segment loss is equivalent to torso injury.

**Reattachment Rules:** Extended range (2.5 body-lengths). Segment-by-segment reattachment possible. Full trunk reconnection requires Sugar Well.

**Cultural Identity:** Patience, underground knowledge, complex social networks.

**Home Region:** Underground networks. Post-Shattering many networks were exposed.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Technique

**Composition Preference:** Soft-Set, Syrup-Core

**Equipment Accommodations:** Requires significant modification or dedicated Wormkin equipment lines.

**Special Rules:** Compression movement. Segment separation is a unique mechanic.

**Starting Benefits:** Extended reattachment range. Compression movement. +1 effective Agility.

**Tradeoffs:** Equipment cost and availability. Elevation disadvantage.

---

### 2.3 Ringborn

**Physical Appearance:** Torus-shaped — a ring or oval loop forming the entire body. Eyes distributed around the ring's outer surface. Limbs extend from the ring.

**Anatomy:** Ring body (single connected region) + limbs (2–4).

**Movement Type:** Rolling movement primary (high speed on flat ground). Upright bipedal on limbs (slower). Ring rotation presents different surfaces.

**Limb Layout:** Ring body (cannot be separated in sections; fracture heals at Sugar Well only), limbs (2–4, reattachable).

**Reattachment Rules:** Limbs standard. Ring fracture requires Sugar Well or Gelomancer Mender.

**Cultural Identity:** Cyclical thinking, formal structure, hierarchy.

**Home Region:** Crystalline Highlands (PROPOSED).

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Spirit

**Composition Preference:** Crystal-Set, Firm-Set

**Equipment Accommodations:** Custom armor for ring anatomy required. Limb equipment standard.

**Special Rules:** Roll-dodge. Ring body natural armor. Ring fracture is catastrophic.

**Starting Benefits:** Roll-dodge in combat. +1 effective Agility.

**Tradeoffs:** Ring fracture is catastrophic. Custom armor expensive. Rolling limited in tight spaces.

### 2.4 Gumdrop Folk

**Physical Appearance:** Classic teardrop silhouette. Rounded base, pointed head. Short legs. Smooth shiny surface. Aerated composition shows internal bubbles.

**Anatomy:** Single-body teardrop. Head is the pointed top; body/torso is the rounded whole. Short limbs from sides.

**Movement Type:** Ground bipedal (short legs). Excellent lateral stability. Can hop.

**Limb Layout:** Head (reattachable), body/torso (unified, not separable), arms (2, reattachable), legs (2, short, reattachable).

**Reattachment Rules:** Standard. Aerated composition accelerates bonding.

**Cultural Identity:** Community-oriented, oral tradition, warmth.

**Home Region:** Distributed; concentrated in Fractured Flats post-Shattering.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Fortitude, Spirit

**Composition Preference:** Aerated, Soft-Set

**Equipment Accommodations:** Short stature requires some weapon modifications. Common equipment widely available.

**Special Rules:** Low center of gravity — reduced knockback. Hop movement.

**Starting Benefits:** Enhanced knockback resistance. +1 effective Fortitude.

**Tradeoffs:** Shorter reach. Lower base movement speed.

---

### 2.5 Jellybean Folk

**Physical Appearance:** Smooth, oval, slightly flattened. Color correlates strongly with flavor affinity. Thinner, more fluid-looking limbs.

**Anatomy:** Standard bipedal. Smooth head-torso transition (no distinct neck region structurally).

**Movement Type:** Ground bipedal. Above-average lateral agility.

**Limb Layout:** Head (semi-integrated with torso, reattachable), torso (not separable), arms (2, reattachable), legs (2, reattachable).

**Reattachment Rules:** Fast standard. Crystal-Set slightly slower.

**Cultural Identity:** Mercurial, emotionally transparent, academic inclination.

**Home Region:** Distributed; Sugar Coast concentration.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Intellect

**Composition Preference:** Crystal-Set, Layered

**Equipment Accommodations:** Close to standard proportions; minor modifications only.

**Special Rules:** Lateral agility bonus. Head/torso integration reduces head-hit severity.

**Starting Benefits:** Lateral dodge bonus. +1 effective Agility.

**Tradeoffs:** Lighter mass — susceptible to knockback and Crush.

---

### 2.6 Bottleborn

**Physical Appearance:** Bottle-shaped — wide base body, narrowing neck, head at top. Transparency varies by composition. Stopper cap optional (cosmetic).

**Anatomy:** Neck is a distinct, fragile-but-flexible structural region.

**Movement Type:** Ground bipedal, standard. Head rotation up to 270°.

**Limb Layout:** Head (reattachable), neck (distinct region, reattachable with precision requirement), torso (not separable), arms (2, reattachable), legs (2, reattachable).

**Reattachment Rules:** Neck reattachment +30% time (precision alignment required) or Gelomancer/Sugar Well.

**Cultural Identity:** Mercantile pragmatism, deal-orientation. Built the pre-Shattering trade networks.

**Home Region:** Sugar Coast.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Intellect, Technique

**Composition Preference:** Syrup-Core, Layered

**Equipment Accommodations:** Specialized neck armor. Otherwise standard.

**Special Rules:** Extended head rotation (wide detection arc). Neck is a designed weak point.

**Starting Benefits:** Awareness bonus. +1 effective Intellect. Small currency bonus.

**Tradeoffs:** Neck structural vulnerability. Neck armor expensive.

---

### 2.7 Sourlings

**Physical Appearance:** Visibly sour-coated — crystalline powder-like surface texture. Colors tend toward yellow-green.

**Anatomy:** Standard bipedal. Sour coating is a distinct outer layer over gel interior (natural armor layer).

**Movement Type:** Ground bipedal, standard.

**Limb Layout:** Standard. All reattachable.

**Reattachment Rules:** Slightly extended window (coating stabilizes separated limbs). Faster in Sour environments.

**Cultural Identity:** Endurance culture, dark humor.

**Home Region:** Sourswamp Delta.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Fortitude, Technique

**Composition Preference:** Firm-Set, Crystal-Set (uncommon)

**Equipment Accommodations:** Standard. Sour-resistant materials preferred.

**Special Rules:** Sour damage resistance. Sour status accumulates more slowly.

**Starting Benefits:** Sour resistance. Faster Stamina recovery. +1 effective Fortitude.

**Tradeoffs:** Sour coating reduces Adhesion slightly. Cultural friction with some lineages.

### 2.8 Licoricefolk

**Physical Appearance:** Dark, ropy, flexible. Distinctly licorice texture rather than fruit gel. Dark colors dominant.

**Anatomy:** Variable body plans. Standard biped template most common. Prehensile sections possible (cosmetic).

**Movement Type:** Ground movement with constriction. Excellent in close quarters.

**Limb Layout:** Standard limbs (reattachable). Prehensile sections regenerate. Limb resonance sensing at range.

**Reattachment Rules:** Standard + body resonance sensing (PROPOSED: automatic limb awareness within 4 body-lengths).

**Cultural Identity:** Layered trust, privacy, complex social architecture.

**Home Region:** Underground networks. Post-Shattering many were exposed.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Intellect

**Composition Preference:** Soft-Set, Layered

**Equipment Accommodations:** Licorice-specific armor. Standard requires modification.

**Special Rules:** Constriction grapple bonus. Limb sensing. Compression movement.

**Starting Benefits:** +1 effective Agility. Constriction bonus. Compression movement.

**Tradeoffs:** Specialized equipment. Unusual flavor-licorice interactions.

### 2.9 Marshfolk

**Physical Appearance:** Large, broad-based, water-resistant outer layer. Slightly duller surface. Wide stance. Earth-tone colors.

**Anatomy:** Standard bipedal with wider stance and adapted foot structure.

**Movement Type:** Ground bipedal (above average in wet/soft terrain, slightly below on hard). Swimming in viscous fluids.

**Limb Layout:** Standard bipedal. All reattachable. Foot is a distinct sub-region.

**Reattachment Rules:** Standard. Faster in wet environments.

**Cultural Identity:** Literal, practical, deeply community-bound.

**Home Region:** Sourswamp Delta and syrup-river regions.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Fortitude, Might

**Composition Preference:** Soft-Set, Syrup-Core

**Equipment Accommodations:** Water-resistant treatment preferred.

**Special Rules:** Terrain mastery (wet/soft). Swimming in viscous fluids.

**Starting Benefits:** Terrain mastery. +1 effective Fortitude. Stability bonus on soft terrain.

**Tradeoffs:** Terrain advantage only in home terrain type.

### 2.10 Jawbreaker Kin

**Physical Appearance:** Small, nearly spherical, extremely hard crystallized exterior. Face embedded in shell. Dense and opaque.

**Anatomy:** Spherical body — head and torso unified (the shell is the whole body). Limbs extend from sphere.

**Movement Type:** Ground bipedal (short, strong limbs). Can roll (slow, precise). Excellent at holding position.

**Limb Layout:** Shell body (unified head-torso, not separable — damage accumulates on shell), arms (2, short, strong, reattachable), legs (2, short, strong, reattachable).

**Reattachment Rules:** PROPOSED +25% base reattachment time due to hard shell.

**Cultural Identity:** Endurance-through-silence, terse, respect by action.

**Home Region:** Mountain ranges (now floating fragments).

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Fortitude, Stability (derived stat directly boosted)

**Composition Preference:** Crystal-Set, Firm-Set

**Equipment Accommodations:** Shell armor specialized. Limb equipment standard.

**Special Rules:** Shell Defense (natural armor). Crystallizing resistance. Harder to body-region target.

**Starting Benefits:** Natural armor. +1 effective Fortitude. Stability bonus.

**Tradeoffs:** Lowest Agility tendency. Reattachment delay. Adhesion interference.

### 2.11 Drakegums

**Physical Appearance:** Large, draconic, scale-patterned. Raised crystalline-gel scales over softer interior. Wings optional (cosmetic). Tail always present. Jewel-tone colors dominant.

**Anatomy:** Large bipedal, scale plating, tail, optional wings.

**Movement Type:** Ground bipedal. Glide from elevation (cosmetic wings — not powered flight). Tail provides balance.

**Limb Layout:** Head (reattachable), torso/body (scale-covered, not separable), arms (2, reattachable), legs (2, reattachable), tail (reattachable), wings (cosmetic, regenerate at Sugar Wells).

**Reattachment Rules:** PROPOSED +15% base reattachment time (scale surface complicates bonding).

**Cultural Identity:** Noble traditions, territorial pride, archaic formal grammar in some dialects.

**Home Region:** Ashglass range.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Might, Spirit

**Composition Preference:** Layered, Crystal-Set

**Equipment Accommodations:** Scale-compatible armor required. Specialized and expensive.

**Special Rules:** Scale Resistance (vs Slice/Pierce). Tail Balance (Stability bonus). Glide.

**Starting Benefits:** Scale Resistance. +1 effective Might. Spirit resonance.

**Tradeoffs:** Largest target profile. Wings restrict armor options. Scale armor expensive.

### 2.12 Swarmkin

**Physical Appearance:** Colony of smaller gummy entities operating as one person. Tight humanoid form or loose dispersed swarm.

**Anatomy:** Colony anatomy — dense head concentration, anterior/core/limb masses.

**Movement Type:** Humanoid bipedal (tight form). Swarm movement (dispersed). Limited dispersed flight.

**Limb Layout:** Colony formations — "limb loss" is dispersal, not separation. Colony recall replaces reattachment. Sugar Well restores full colony count.

**Reattachment Rules:** N/A — colony recovery rules apply.

**Cultural Identity:** Collective consensus. Individual personality within collective identity.

**Home Region:** Distributed; often underrepresented areas.

**Primary Attribute Tendency:** Agility, Spirit

**Composition Preference:** Aerated, Soft-Set (per colony member)

**Equipment Accommodations:** Swarmkin-specific. Conventional armor does not fit dispersed form.

**Special Rules:** Colony dispersal for traversal and damage resistance. Unique movement. Wide area awareness.

**Starting Benefits:** Colony dispersal. Area awareness. +1 effective Agility.

**Tradeoffs:** All equipment specialized. Environmental extremes affect whole colony.

## 3. Compositions — Full Detail

### 3.1 Soft-Set

**Description:** Standard, fully flexible gel. Default gel state for most lineages. Bodies deform readily under impact and spring back. Most forgiving for reattachment.

**Lore:** Comfortable in moderate temperatures. Extreme cold firms; extreme heat can melt.

**Mechanical Effects:** Best deformation recovery. Best reattachment speed. Good Crush resistance (absorbs). Moderate structural integrity.

**Damage Type Interactions:**
- Crush: Reduced (absorbs rather than fractures)
- Slice: Normal
- Heat: Increased deformation (melting)
- Cold: Temporary stiffness (toward Crystal-Set effect — reversible)

**Environmental:** Functions well in most environments. Wet improves Adhesion. Extreme cold causes stiffness.

---

### 3.2 Firm-Set

**Description:** Denser, stiffer gel. Better baseline structural resistance than Soft-Set. Less deformation under impact.

**Lore:** Firm-Set ages toward Crystal-Set under prolonged stress and cold — slowly and partially reversibly.

**Mechanical Effects:** Higher structural integrity. Lower deformation. Slower reattachment. Good resistance to Slice and Crush. Moderate Heat vulnerability.

**Damage Type Interactions:**
- Crush: Normal (takes rather than absorbs)
- Slice: Reduced (denser surface)
- Heat: Normal vulnerability
- Cold: Reduced vulnerability (already firm)

**Environmental:** Excellent in cold. Heat softens toward Soft-Set temporarily.

---

### 3.3 Crystal-Set

**Description:** Crystallized outer or full gel structure. Highest natural armor. Brittle against shattering forces.

**Lore:** Develops naturally in older creatures, cold-environment-adapted lineages, or heavy Lattice exposure. Advanced Crystal-Set individuals are Staleborn.

**Mechanical Effects:** Highest natural armor (Slice/Pierce). Brittle against Crush/Cold. Slow reattachment (hard surfaces bond poorly). High Stability. Low Adhesion.

**Damage Type Interactions:**
- Crush: Significantly increased (shattering risk)
- Slice: Greatly reduced
- Pierce: Reduced
- Cold: Shattering risk at extremes
- Heat: Gradual softening

**Environmental:** Excellent in dry, stable environments. Cold extremes risk shattering. Heat softens.

---

### 3.4 Syrup-Core

**Description:** Liquid or semi-liquid interior with thin, compliant outer layer. Most fluid composition. Fast reattachment. Lowest structural integrity.

**Lore:** Associated with deep Lattice exposure. Sugar Wells sometimes produce Syrup-Core individuals.

**Mechanical Effects:** Fastest reattachment. Lowest structural integrity. Can partially flow through gaps. Vulnerable to all damage. High Adhesion.

**Damage Type Interactions:**
- All types: Moderate to high vulnerability
- Sour: Particularly dangerous (destabilizes internal fluid)
- Heat: Rapid deformation
- Cold: Internal crystallization risk

**Environmental:** Poor in temperature extremes. Excellent in wet/fluid environments.

---

### 3.5 Layered

**Description:** Distinct outer and inner material layers with different properties. Two phases of damage response.

**Lore:** Develops through deliberate gel chemistry, regional mineral exposure, or lineage-specific biology (Drakegums develop scale layering naturally).

**Mechanical Effects:** Outer layer tracked separately from inner. When outer depleted, inner exposed with different properties. Three effective damage reduction layers (outer + inner + armor).

**Damage Type Interactions:** Vary by outer layer composition type. Reattachment rate depends on which layer was severed.

**Environmental:** Outer layer may have specific environmental affinities.

---

### 3.6 Aerated

**Description:** Gas-bubble-infused gel. Lighter than standard. Bounces readily. Unusual kinetic absorption. Common in Gumdrop Folk.

**Lore:** Results from air incorporation during gel formation. Some Aerated individuals can control internal air pressure.

**Mechanical Effects:** Lowest mass. Bounce recovery (knockback becomes acrobatic). Crush resistance. Heat vulnerability (bubble expansion). Limb separation easier (reduced gel cohesion).

**Damage Type Interactions:**
- Crush: Significantly reduced (bubble absorption)
- Slice: Normal to slightly increased (reduced surface tension)
- Heat: Increased (bubble expansion — explosive risk)
- Cold: Stiffens (toward Firm-Set effect)

**Environmental:** Best in moderate temperatures. Heat-dangerous.

## 4. Flavor Affinities — Full Detail

### 4.1 Cherry Ember

**Energetic Quality:** Warm, combustible, internally generated heat. Bodies run slightly warmer — detectable by touch, occasionally visible as faint warm glow.

**Lore:** Dominant affinity of the pre-Shattering military tradition. Sugar Guard drew heavily from Cherry Ember Bearkin.

**Affinity:** Heat damage application and enhancement
**Resistance:** Heat damage (PROPOSED: 20% reduction)
**Vulnerability:** Cold damage slightly increased

**Combat:** Melee attacks carry minor Heat with Spirit investment. Enhanced cold-environment performance. Burning buildup more effective.

---

### 4.2 Blue Frost

**Energetic Quality:** Cool, crystallizing, externally projectable cold. Bodies cooler than ambient — surfaces near them condense.

**Lore:** Associated with Crystalline Highlands. Common in Jellybean Folk, Ringborn, Jawbreaker Kin.

**Affinity:** Cold damage
**Resistance:** Cold damage
**Vulnerability:** Heat damage slightly increased

**Combat:** Freezing buildup. Crystallization acceleration. Excellent in cold environments.

---

### 4.3 Lime Sour

**Energetic Quality:** Caustic, reactive, chemical dissolution properties. Slightly corrosive surface contact quality.

**Lore:** Dominant affinity of Sourling culture. Associated with Licorice Rogue tradition.

**Affinity:** Sour damage
**Resistance:** Sour damage
**Vulnerability:** Sugar damage slightly increased

**Combat:** Sourrot buildup. Armor corrosion over time. Enhanced vs Crystal-Set opponents.

---

### 4.4 Golden Sugar

**Energetic Quality:** Radiant, stabilizing, restorative. Warm glow. Naturally stabilizing presence — nearby gel tends toward stability.

**Lore:** Associated with Temple Pourer tradition and Sugar Well proximity.

**Affinity:** Sugar damage (healing and harm)
**Resistance:** Sugar damage
**Vulnerability:** Sour damage slightly increased

**Combat:** Sugar damage applies minor restoration to wielder. Enhanced healing interactions. Ward stabilization bonus.

---

### 4.5 Cola Fizz

**Energetic Quality:** Volatile, kinetic, explosive-adjacent. Internal pressure releases suddenly under stress — fizzing kinetic burst.

**Lore:** Arena combat tradition has disproportionate Cola Fizz practitioners.

**Affinity:** Shock damage
**Resistance:** Shock damage
**Vulnerability:** Internal pressure cost (self-damage risk if overused)

**Combat:** Shock application. Momentum enhancement. Pressure management trade-off.

---

### 4.6 Grape Shade

**Energetic Quality:** Dark, Spirit-adjacent, resonant with spiritual and dimensional forces. Interacts with ward energy readily.

**Lore:** Associated with Wrapperborn culture and spiritual traditions.

**Affinity:** Spirit damage
**Resistance:** Spirit damage
**Vulnerability:** Shock damage slightly increased (kinetic disrupts spiritual energy)

**Combat:** Spirit damage. Ward interaction bonus (stronger Wards). Detection of spirit-sensitive phenomena.

---

### 4.7 Peach Bloom

**Energetic Quality:** Adhesive, gentle, healing-adjacent. Naturally higher Adhesion quality — bonds well with surfaces and bodies.

**Lore:** Mender tradition within Gelomancy strongly associates with Peach Bloom.

**Affinity:** Adhesion enhancement (not a direct damage type)
**Resistance:** Sour damage (Adhesion buffer)
**Vulnerability:** Cold damage (cold reduces Adhesion)

**Special:** No direct offensive damage type. Enhances all Adhesion-dependent mechanics. Reattachment faster. Bind abilities more effective.

---

### 4.8 Lemon Spark

**Energetic Quality:** Electric, sharp, analytically precise. Detectable electrical-adjacent quality — slightly sharp to touch, occasional discharge.

**Lore:** Gelwright Circle has disproportionate Lemon Spark members — precision engineering resonance.

**Affinity:** Shock damage (precision-focused vs Cola Fizz explosive)
**Resistance:** Shock damage
**Vulnerability:** Wet environments increase vulnerability and application

**Combat:** Precision Shock damage. Analysis bonus (Monster Knowledge faster in combat). Technique interactions.

---

### 4.9 Clear Neutral

**Energetic Quality:** Unaligned, adaptive. No dominant flavor resonance — product of complex flavor environments.

**Lore:** Most common in urban, multi-cultural communities.

**Affinity:** No primary type
**Resistance:** All types at small flat value (PROPOSED: 5% across all)
**Vulnerability:** None specific

**Special:** Better interaction with enchantments with no affinity requirement. Wider effective range of flavor consumables (any affinity consumable at full effectiveness).
