## What Shattercrown Is

**[VERIFIED]** Shattercrown is GUMFALL's opt-in full-PvP city. It is a single geographic fragment where the normal rules of protected zones and consent-gated combat are suspended within district boundaries.

**[VERIFIED]** Shattercrown is not the default experience of GUMFALL. It is a zone players choose to enter, understanding that combat against other players can occur at any time, that material items designated as "at risk" can be lost, and that the political and economic ecology of the city is shaped entirely by player activity.

**[PROPOSED]** Shattercrown functions simultaneously as:
- A full-PvP risk/reward environment for players seeking high-stakes conflict.
- An economic hub where rare and contested materials are traded, stolen, and transformed.
- A political arena where guilds, factions, and individuals compete for the Tyrant title.
- A PvE-adjacent space where environmental threats and NPC factions provide context for the conflict.

**[DERIVED]** All VERIFIED PvP design commitments apply in Shattercrown: no pay-to-win, no permanent progression loss, no paid cosmetic drops. See [PvP Foundations](./PVP_FOUNDATIONS.md).

## Consent and Entry Requirements

**[VERIFIED]** Entering Shattercrown requires explicit consent and a minimum character level.

### Consent Process
**[PROPOSED]**
- The entrance to Shattercrown is a specific gate or crossing point, not an ambient region boundary.
- First-time entry triggers a consent confirmation explaining full-PvP zone terms, "at risk" definition, and what items cannot be taken.
- Players may disable the confirmation screen on subsequent entries after demonstrating comprehension on first entry.
- Players can exit at any time using the extraction system (see Extraction Mechanics). Extraction is not instant and may be interrupted.

### Minimum Level
**[VERIFIED]** A minimum character level is required. **[ASSUMPTION]** Minimum Level 20 as a working assumption — requires validation against the level range at which characters have sufficient depth to participate meaningfully.

### Risk Pouch Designation
**[PROPOSED]** Before entering, players designate the contents of their Risk Pouch — items they bring in that can be lost. Items not in the Risk Pouch are protected.

### Protection Zones
**[PROPOSED]** The Broken Gate entrance area has a brief protection period. Certain designated safe rooms in the Bloodmarket permit temporary non-combat status for trade. Outside these zones, PvP is unrestricted.

## Districts of Shattercrown

**[VERIFIED]** Shattercrown has six districts.

### District 1: Broken Gate

**[PROPOSED]** The entry point to Shattercrown, framed by the enormous shattered pre-Shattering gateway that gives the fragment its name.

**Gameplay Function:**
- Entry and exit processing. Risk Pouch designation occurs here.
- Brief temporary protection window on entry for orientation.
- Lower-intensity conflict zone by social convention — not enforced by the game.
- NPC faction presence including Notoriety tracking posts and the Bounty board.

**Atmosphere:** Functional, worn, perpetually busy. The gate structure is maintained for structural safety but not repaired aesthetically. Everything has the quality of a place people pass through rather than settle.

---

### District 2: Bloodmarket

**[PROPOSED]** Shattercrown's primary commercial district and the most dangerous market in the Sweetlands.

**Gameplay Function:**
- Direct player-to-player trade of Shattercrown-obtained materials and contested items.
- Limited temporary safe-room zones for trade negotiations.
- NPC vendor presence for Shattercrown-specific goods.
- Robbery is possible in all but the safe rooms. Trade inside safe rooms is protected; trade in the open market is not.
- Rare materials concentrate here because the economic density compresses prices toward actual market value without protection-zone inflation.

**Atmosphere:** Dense, loud, layered in competing sensory noise. Architecture is improvised and multi-story — stalls built atop stalls, structures cantilevered over alleys.

---

### District 3: Severed Plaza

**[PROPOSED]** Shattercrown's open conflict district — the area where PvP combat is most concentrated.

**Gameplay Function:**
- Primary combat arena. No objectives, no match structure — open combat at any time.
- Environmental micro-terrain: elevated positions, chokepoints, cover structures.
- Tyrant of Shattercrown title defended in the Severed Plaza during challenge windows.
- King of the Hill weekly event centered here.

**Atmosphere:** An open clearing in the city's dense construction. The plaza floor shows the evidence of every major battle — scorch marks, structural damage, replacement candy-stone, accumulated wear of sustained conflict.

---

### District 4: Syrup Sewers

**[PROPOSED]** Shattercrown's underground sub-district, accessible through grates, broken infrastructure, and known but hidden entrances.

**Gameplay Function:**
- The stealth layer: lower visibility, narrower passages, sound-dampening syrup-slicked walls.
- Refuge for high-Notoriety players and Bounty targets.
- Guild objective content involving Sewer-level caches and control points.
- Underground traversal routes connecting all districts, bypassing district-level surface combat.
- Environmental hazard: syrup adhesion variation. Adhesion-dependent movement more effective here.

**Atmosphere:** Dark, narrow, disorienting by design. Faint bioluminescent syrup glow in some sections. Navigation requires spatial memory or awareness tools.

---

### District 5: Broken Palace

**[PROPOSED]** Shattercrown's center of political power — the ruined administrative structure of whoever last held formal governance before Shattercrown became what it is.

**Gameplay Function:**
- Primary location for large-scale guild objective content.
- Throne room of the Broken Palace is the Tyrant's seat.
- Mid-to-high intensity PvP zone. Elevated position and defensible architecture have tactical value.
- Some collapsed sections create class-relevant traversal variation.
- Guild Siege Week content centers on the Broken Palace.

**Atmosphere:** Grand ruin. Original architecture visible beneath modifications, repairs, and improvisations of contested occupation. Banners from multiple guilds mark different wings and towers. Nothing is clean; everything is contested.

---

### District 6: Empty Throne

**[PROPOSED]** The Broken Palace throne room — the exact center of Shattercrown's political symbolism.

**Gameplay Function:**
- Location of Tyrant challenge events.
- Limited-access: reaching the Empty Throne requires navigating the contested palace interior.
- High-consequence loot cache spawns in the Empty Throne area on a schedule.
- Weekly event interactions activate Throne-area content.

**Atmosphere:** Deliberate emptiness that is louder than presence would be. The throne is intact, ornate, and unoccupied. Every surface shows evidence of every faction that has held it temporarily.

## Risk Pouch System

**[VERIFIED]** Players designate what items they bring into Shattercrown as the Risk Pouch — items at risk of loss if defeated.

### How It Works
**[PROPOSED]**
- Before entering, players use a Risk Pouch interface to mark specific items as "at risk."
- At-risk items can be looted by a victorious opponent on defeat.
- Items NOT marked at-risk cannot be looted under any circumstances.
- Risk Pouch has a capacity limit (weight or slot-based — exact system to be determined).

### Strategic Implications
**[PROPOSED]**
- Players bringing nothing at-risk have nothing to lose but receive no Risk Pouch reward bonus on victory.
- Players bringing high-value items at risk receive better material rewards on successful combat outcomes.
- The reward-vs-loss calculation creates genuine risk management decisions.

### Visibility
**[PROPOSED]** Risk Pouch contents are not perfectly visible to other players. General weight signals or obvious presence may be observable, but not exact itemization. Prevents perfect information from eliminating all engagement decisions.

## Extraction Mechanics

**[PROPOSED]** Extraction is the mechanism by which players leave Shattercrown safely, retaining Risk Pouch contents.

### Base Extraction
- Players reach the Broken Gate exit and activate the extraction process, beginning a wind-up timer.
- During the wind-up, the player is visible, stationary, and vulnerable to interruption.
- If hit by another player's attack, extraction is cancelled and must be restarted.
- If extraction completes uninterrupted, the player exits and Risk Pouch is retained.

### Extraction Tools
**[PROPOSED]**
- Shattercrown-obtainable items can accelerate extraction timers.
- Group extraction: coordinated but a larger visible target.
- Emergency extraction: usable from anywhere in Shattercrown (not just Broken Gate), takes longer, still interruptible.

### Death Instead of Extraction
**[PROPOSED]** If a player dies without extracting, Risk Pouch items are available for looting by the player who delivered the killing blow, or by the first player to reach the body within a loot window. Protected inventory items are never available.

## Bounty System

**[PROPOSED]** The Bounty system tracks and rewards the elimination of targeted players.

### How Bounties Are Created
- Players who repeatedly defeat others in Shattercrown accumulate Notoriety. At certain thresholds, a bounty is automatically placed.
- Players can manually place bounties on specific players using Shattercrown currency.
- The Bounty Board in the Broken Gate district displays active bounties with general location information but not exact coordinates.

### Bounty Rewards
- Eliminating a bounty target yields the bounty reward — Shattercrown currency and materials appropriate to the target's Notoriety level.
- The bounty is consumed on elimination.
- The eliminated player's Notoriety is reduced on bounty completion.

### Targeting Information
**[PROPOSED]** Bounty tracking provides general district-level location (e.g., "last seen in Syrup Sewers") updated periodically, not continuously. Players still need to find their target.

## Notoriety System

**[PROPOSED]** Notoriety measures a player's historical combat impact within Shattercrown in the current session.

### Accumulation
- Defeating other players increases Notoriety.
- Defeating higher-Notoriety players provides larger increases.
- Defeating players with active bounties provides additional Notoriety.

### Effects of High Notoriety
- Bounties placed automatically at specific thresholds.
- Periodic visible pulses that communicate general presence of high-Notoriety players.
- Better loot rewards from Shattercrown content.
- Eligibility for Tyrant challenges.

### Reduction
- Being defeated reduces Notoriety significantly.
- Successful extraction partially reduces Notoriety (cashing out).
- Notoriety resets between Shattercrown sessions.

## Guild Objective System

**[PROPOSED]** Guilds active in Shattercrown can pursue guild-specific objectives that provide guild-level rewards and contribute to inter-guild standing.

### Types of Guild Objectives
- **Territory Control:** Hold a specific district control point for a duration against all challengers.
- **Extraction Goals:** Extract a total volume of Risk Pouch material across a session.
- **Elimination Targets:** Eliminate a specific number of opposing guild members.
- **Cache Recovery:** Locate and extract specific Shattercrown-spawning cache items before other guilds do.

### Guild Rewards
**[PROPOSED]** Guild objective completion contributes to:
- Guild-level standing in the Shattercrown ecology.
- Guild treasury resources.
- Progress toward Guild Siege Week content (see [Weekly Events](./WEEKLY_EVENTS.md)).
- Ceremonial recognition — guild names on Broken Palace signage for top-performing guilds.

**[DERIVED]** Guild rewards do not include exclusive power items, mandatory-progression items, or anything that creates an unbridgeable power gap.

## Tyrant of Shattercrown

**[VERIFIED]** The Tyrant of Shattercrown is a player-held title with ceremonial and governance benefits.

### How to Become Tyrant
**[PROPOSED]**
- The title is held by the player or group that controls the Empty Throne district.
- Challenge windows open on a schedule during which the Empty Throne is contestable.
- The current Tyrant must defend the Empty Throne during challenge windows.
- A challenger who defeats the Tyrant or holds the Empty Throne at challenge window end becomes the new Tyrant.

### Tyrant Benefits
**[VERIFIED]** Tyrant benefits are ceremonial and governance-based. They do not include:
- **[VERIFIED]** XP multipliers.
- Normalized-PvP advantages.
- Direct combat stat bonuses.

**[PROPOSED]** Tyrant benefits include:
- Visible title and cosmetic identifier active while in Shattercrown.
- Governance voice: ability to set some cosmetic configurations for the Empty Throne area.
- Passive currency flow from Shattercrown's economic activity while holding the title.
- Notoriety amplification for successful defenses.
- Historical record as a former Tyrant.

### PvE Equivalent
**[PROPOSED]** For players who prefer PvE, equivalent recognition systems exist through Mythic Sanctum completion and Monster Ascension Tournament performance. Shattercrown Tyrant is not the only path to the game's highest-recognition status.

## Crowd Scent Anti-Zerg Rule

**[VERIFIED]** The Crowd Scent anti-zerg mechanic prevents infinite stacking in Shattercrown.

**[PROPOSED]** How it works:
- Groups accumulate a "Crowd Scent" value scaling with group size.
- Above a threshold, Crowd Scent becomes visible to other players in the same district.
- Beyond the threshold, environmental and NPC factors actively oppose the large group: Shattercrown's ambient NPC threat level scales, Notoriety gain per-member is reduced, and other mechanical discouragements activate.
- Exact threshold values and scaling factors to be determined through balance testing.

**Rationale:** Without Crowd Scent, Shattercrown would collapse into a pure numbers game eliminating skill and individual expression. Crowd Scent provides structural resistance to numerical advantage without making large group play impossible.

**[PROPOSED]** Crowd Scent does not prevent group play. It prevents unlimited scaling advantage. A coordinated smaller group can still defeat a larger group.

## Underdog Rewards

**[VERIFIED]** Underdog rewards compensate for unfavorable numbers in Shattercrown.

**[PROPOSED]** Mechanics:
- A player or small group that defeats a larger group (defined by numbers differential) receives an Underdog bonus on top of normal combat rewards.
- Underdog bonus scales with the numbers differential.
- Underdog status is calculated at engagement start and displayed to both sides.

**[PROPOSED]** Underdog rewards contain:
- Elevated Shattercrown currency.
- Elevated material drops from defeated opponents' Risk Pouches.
- Notoriety bonus.
- If part of a bounty or guild objective, a completion bonus multiplier.

**What Underdog rewards are not:**
- Not guaranteed win conditions. The underdog still has to win to receive the bonus.
- Not compensation for losing. The Underdog bonus only activates on win.

**Rationale:** Creates incentive for skilled smaller groups to seek out and engage larger groups, turning numerical advantage into a double-edged consideration.

## What Never Drops

**[VERIFIED]** Certain categories of items are never at risk and cannot be taken from a defeated player under any circumstances.

### Never Drops
**[VERIFIED]**
- **Bound gear:** Items bound to the player cannot be placed in the Risk Pouch and cannot be looted.
- **Permanent progression items:** Character level, skill points, attribute investments — no character progression can be lost in Shattercrown.
- **Paid cosmetics:** Any item acquired through real-money purchase is permanently protected.
- **Essential quest items:** Items required to complete main story or essential progression quests are protected.

### Always Drops (if in Risk Pouch)
**[PROPOSED]**
- Freely tradeable crafting materials designated as at-risk.
- Freely tradeable equipment items designated as at-risk.
- Shattercrown-specific currency designated as at-risk.
- Non-quest, non-essential consumables designated as at-risk.

**Rationale:** The Risk Pouch system is the boundary between Shattercrown's risk economy and the player's long-term progression. Protecting permanent progression and paid cosmetics absolutely makes Shattercrown a genuine risk environment without scenarios where core character investment can be removed by PvP.

## Lawless City Economic Ecology

**[PROPOSED]** Shattercrown has a distinct economic ecology.

### Supply Dynamics
- Items in Shattercrown's markets are player-sourced through combat, extraction, and Risk Pouch transfer.
- NPC vendors provide price floors on some materials but do not regulate the player-driven market.
- Rare materials from Royal Courts and Mythic Sanctums concentrate in the Bloodmarket because players who obtained them through high-risk content sell them in the highest-return environment.

### Demand Dynamics
- Crafters who want rare materials have strong motivation to enter Shattercrown as buyers.
- Players who want Shattercrown-specific materials must enter to obtain them.
- The Tyrant title's currency flow means political players invest in Shattercrown for the economic return.

### Economic Violence
**[PROPOSED]** Attacking traders, robbing caravans, intercepting extractions — these are legitimate Shattercrown activities, part of the designed economy. Players who want to trade safely have the safe room option. Players who accept the risk of open market trade can choose that. Both are valid strategies.

### Notoriety as Currency
**[PROPOSED]** High Notoriety functions as a form of social currency — it signals success in combat, making the player simultaneously a valuable bounty target and a dangerous opponent. This dual nature creates meaningful social dynamics.

## Lore and Narrative Context

**[PROPOSED]** Shattercrown's fragment was once a genuine administrative and governance center. Whatever it was, it fell.

The history of Shattercrown's fall is disputed:
- **Crown Accord version:** A cautionary example of what happens when governance is abandoned.
- **Free Pour version:** A proof of concept that communities organize their own conflict without royal structures.
- **Memory Keepers version:** Shattercrown fell because specific post-Shattering governance mechanisms failed in documented ways unrelated to ideology.

**[UNKNOWN]** The true history of Shattercrown is an owner decision. The competing narratives above are a design framework; resolution determines what players discover through the world's knowledge systems.

**[PROPOSED]** The Empty Throne and Broken Palace architecture contains lore artifacts providing pieces of pre-Shattercrown history. A complete lore collection run is a distinct achievement path that does not require combat success — it rewards exploration and knowledge engagement in a conflict environment.

**Faction Relationships [PROPOSED]:**
- **Crown Accord:** Nominally considers the fragment Crown Accord territory but does not enforce this inside Shattercrown.
- **Free Pour:** Ideological investment in Shattercrown as post-royal social organization; also profits economically from its markets.
- **Gelwright Circle:** Research presence in Broken Gate district studying Shattercrown as a social and physical experiment.
- **Memory Keepers:** Historical documentation of pre-fall period. Archival, not political.
- **Void Wrappers:** Activity suspected but unconfirmed.
