# Equipment and Economy

## Equipment Model

Every item is defined by:

- Form
- Material
- Construction
- Craftsmanship
- Condition
- Provenance

Equipment primarily comes from what an enemy actually carries, manufactures, or
protects.

## Material Progression

Representative progression includes:

1. Natural materials
2. Copper and bronze
3. Iron
4. Steel and articulated plate
5. Advanced alloys and monster materials
6. Mithril
7. Adamantine, orichalcum, and platinum alloy
8. Iridium
9. Aetherium and Prismatic Sugarsteel
10. Singular and Transcendent materials

## Currency

The economy uses a base-ten metal standard:

- Tin
- Copper
- Bronze
- Silver
- Electrum
- Gold
- Platinum
- Iridium
- Aetherium

## Weapon Enchantments

Eligible weapons may very rarely possess one to four natural enchantments.

A four-Grand-Enchantment weapon is called Malady.

## Armor Wards

Armor Wards counter enchantment buildup according to equipment strength,
coverage, material, condition, and level overmatch. A greatly stronger enchanted
weapon can overwhelm obsolete defensive Wards.
