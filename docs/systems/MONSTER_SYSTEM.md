# Monster System

Each monster is composed from:

- Creature family
- Anatomy
- Composition
- Flavor affinity
- Combat role
- Intelligence
- Temperament
- Rank
- Faction
- Loot ecology

Major breakable anatomy must change monster behavior.

Monster ranks include:

- Minion
- Standard
- Veteran
- Elite
- Champion
- Boss
- Mythic Boss

Boss durability should come from anatomy, armor layers, movement, regeneration
organs, and phase mechanics rather than one oversized health pool.
