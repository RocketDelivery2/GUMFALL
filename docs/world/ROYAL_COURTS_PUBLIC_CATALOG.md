## Overview

The Royal Courts are GUMFALL's named dungeon structures, each ruled by a monstrous sovereign
of exceptional power. This catalog provides the public-facing description of each Royal Court:
its sovereign, its territory, its public lore, and the general type of challenge it presents.

Exact Court layouts, maze solutions, traversal requirements, hidden thresholds, and loot
configurations are protected private design material. See
[PUBLIC_PRIVATE_BOUNDARIES.md](../governance/PUBLIC_PRIVATE_BOUNDARIES.md).

---

## About the Royal Courts

The Royal Courts are ancient structures that predate the current age of the candy world.
Each was built around — or grew up around — a sovereign creature of unusual power and
permanence. The courts are not simply dungeons; they are living political entities. The
sovereign is the court. The court reflects the sovereign.

Royal Courts are designed for groups of 3–6 players (PROPOSED; solo attempts are possible
for experienced players). Each Court has a distinct mechanical and aesthetic identity.

**Public design principle (Core Pillar 9):** Every Royal Court has secrets discoverable
through careful play and observation. None require external resources (guides, wikis) to find —
all clues are present inside the Court. Exact solutions remain protected to preserve the
discovery experience.

## Court Entries

### 1. The Amber Throne — Court of the Sugar Wolf King

**Territory:** The Sugarspire foothills, transitional zone between the Caravan Quarter and the
upper mountain range.

**Sovereign:** Kael the Ungnawed. An ancient Sugar Wolf of exceptional size whose amber
crystallization has spread across his entire form. Unlike ordinary Sugar Wolves (pack predators,
instinctive intelligence), Kael is sapient and territorial. He remembers every hunter who
has entered his court.

**Public Lore:** Kael is said to have driven out three previous dungeon-clearers not through
force but through patience — waiting until they were exhausted, then simply walking them
out of his territory. Hunters who have faced him report that he watches, calculates, and
acts only when advantage is absolute.

**Challenge Character (PROPOSED):** Pack coordination encounter. The Court involves navigation
through den-territory with active pack mechanics. Kael is encountered after the pack has been
addressed. His unique crystallization gives him a combination of soft-set and crystalline
armor properties — standard slice attacks that would separate a normal wolf's limbs are
partially resisted by his crystallization. **PROPOSED design target. NOT PERFORMED: balance validation.**

**Level Band:** PROPOSED 20–30. NOT PERFORMED: final scaling review.

**Publicly Known Loot Theme:** Wolf-material armor components and fanged weapon modifications
tied to pack-hunting styles. Exact drop configurations are private.

### 2. The Spun Court — Court of the Hollow Duchess

**Territory:** The deep Sugarspire Mountains, carved into a formation of hollow-core sugar
crystal pillars.

**Sovereign:** The Hollow Duchess. A creature of uncertain lineage whose body is hollow-spun
composition — essentially a shell of hardened sugar-crystal with an interior that produces
complex resonance effects. Observers report hearing harmonic tones from her court at distance.

**Public Lore:** The Hollow Duchess receives visitors. Unlike other Royal sovereigns who attack
immediately, she allows entry into an outer reception chamber. Whether this represents sapience,
predatory patience, or some instinct-driven territorial ritual is disputed. Some hunters report
hearing her "speak" — not words, but sequences of harmonic tones that certain Gelomancers
claim carry structure.

**Challenge Character (PROPOSED):** Resonance-based encounter. The Court's hollow crystal
architecture creates zones where sound-type and shock-type status effects behave differently.
Environmental navigation and awareness of resonance zones is a component of Court traversal.
**PROPOSED. NOT PERFORMED: gameplay validation.**

**Level Band:** PROPOSED 35–45. NOT PERFORMED: final scaling review.

**Publicly Known Loot Theme:** Hollow-crystal material components, items with resonance
enchantment affinity. Exact drop configurations are private.

### 3. The Sour Throne — Court of the Acid Marshal

**Territory:** The Sour Waste badlands.

**Sovereign:** The Acid Marshal. A creature of the Sour Waste whose body continuously
produces and secretes sour acid. Unlike most monsters who have static body composition,
the Marshal actively alters the material state of its Court's surfaces through continuous
acid secretion.

**Public Lore:** The Marshal's court is said to dissolve intruders slowly rather than
quickly — not out of mercy but because its preferred prey is partially dissolved before
consumption. The Court's surfaces become progressively more acidic with each visit until
a sufficient interval has passed for them to recover.

**Challenge Character (PROPOSED):** Environmental deterioration and acid-type damage
management. Body regions exposed to court surfaces accumulate corrosive status. Equipment
condition degrades in acid zones. The Marshal itself is highly resistant to acid damage.
**PROPOSED. NOT PERFORMED: gameplay validation.**

**Level Band:** PROPOSED 50–60. NOT PERFORMED: final scaling review.

**Publicly Known Loot Theme:** Acid-resistant material components, sour-type enchantment
affinity items. Exact drop configurations are private.

---

### 4. The Crystalline Gauntlet — Court of the Frost Sovereign

**Territory:** Upper Sugarspire Mountains, accessible only through a seasonal approach path
(PROPOSED: seasonal availability tied to world state).

**Sovereign:** The Frost Sovereign. An ancient Jawbreaker-derived creature whose crystallization
has reached a level of permanence that makes it effectively a mobile crystal formation.
Its movement produces cold-type environmental effects in its immediate vicinity.

**Public Lore:** The Frost Sovereign is the oldest known Royal Court sovereign. Hunters who
have returned from its Court describe a creature that appears dormant — until it does not.
Its attack patterns are described as "unhurried" in most accounts and "suddenly total" in
the rest.

**Challenge Character (PROPOSED):** Cold-type combat with crystallization management.
The Court's temperature lowers with depth. Player characters must manage crystallization
accumulation on exposed body regions. The Frost Sovereign's own crystalline armor has
properties that differ from standard crystalline materials.
**PROPOSED. NOT PERFORMED: gameplay validation.**

**Level Band:** PROPOSED 65–80. NOT PERFORMED: final scaling review.

**Publicly Known Loot Theme:** Crystalline armor components of exceptional quality, cold-type
enchantment affinity items. Exact drop configurations are private.
