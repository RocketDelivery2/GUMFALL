# World, Secrets, and Royal Dungeons

The Sweetlands contain:

- Hidden rooms
- Deep dungeon branches
- Royal mazes
- Mythic sanctums
- Unbound secrets

Intelligent monster civilizations may possess kings, queens, courts, royal guards,
treasuries, and concealed throne rooms.

Known design families include:

- Kobold royal warrens
- Hobgoblin military courts
- Gnoll moon courts
- Troll root kingdoms
- Staleborn crypt monarchies
- Dragon throne complexes
- Wrapperborn folded courts

Every significant secret needs multiple fair clue paths. Exact maze solutions,
keys, triggers, and hidden-level clues are private design information.
